# Third-party notices and distribution scope

Spark's original project source, documentation and authored assets are offered
under [MIT](LICENSE), as selected by the repository owner on 2026-10-17.
This grant does not relicense third-party dependencies or caller-provided data.
AI assistance and material-specific provenance are recorded in [PROVENANCE](PROVENANCE.md).

Dependencies are fetched through Cargo; their source and compiled binaries are not
vendored in this source repository. The pinned Cargo manifests and lockfiles name
the dependencies. Preserve each dependency's own licenses/notices when distributing
bundled source, packages or executable binaries; a future binary/package distributor
must review the actual redistributed dependency set and platform components.
There is no claim that every dependency has Spark's MIT license.

Fixed-font previews were rendered with the installed DejaVu Sans Mono font.
No font file is redistributed. Font identity and rendering limitations are recorded
in `previews/render-manifest.json`. No third-party character, sprite sheet or art
pack is included. Historical measured ELF host binaries and raw recordings are
excluded from Git under [the artifact policy](ARTIFACTS.md).

Third-party ownership and license statements in historical evidence remain intact.
