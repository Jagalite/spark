## Code and visual material

Record code/API references independently from art references. A dependency's
license does not license this project. If no third-party source/art was copied or
imported, say so accurately; do not treat that statement, or AI assistance alone,
as proof of legal clearance, ownership, copyright protection, or uniqueness.

For authored assets, link the editable source, deterministic generated Rust data,
schema/version, visual revision, and regeneration-check evidence. Record whether
any external character, sprite sheet, image, texture, font file, or source frame
was used. Do not hide an imported source behind a generated-output label.

## Generated previews

Record the source design and source/configuration hash, seed if applicable, exact
timestamps/input trace, geometry, palette/profile, font/terminal or offscreen
renderer, and capture settings. Distinguish a looping animation from a captured
observation window and a fixed-font derivative from terminal qualification.

List copied/bundled font or encoder assets, if any, separately from installed
local tools. Preserve relevant dependency notices for material actually shipped.
A preview does not establish rights or support claims absent from its source.

Use “not recorded,” “not independently verified,” or “pending” when accurate.
Never invent an author, license, approval, hash, test, measurement, or review result.
